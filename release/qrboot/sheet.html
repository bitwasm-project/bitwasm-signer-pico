Bitcoin signer for inheritance

This sheet holds no secret. The key is the SeedQR on a metal plate kept elsewhere. Copy this sheet as often as you like.

You need: a phone with a browser, the SeedQR plate, and a wallet app that makes the transaction (Sparrow or similar)

Loader: https://bitwasm-project.github.io/bitwasm-signer-pico/qrboot/

SHA-256 of the signer (compare with the screen in step 3)

c947 fc75 3237 7138 e62f 925b d85b 45b1 5d41 5612 42f5 77d8 c852 602c 5355 c3a7

  1. Open the loader above once while online, then put the phone in airplane mode. Once opened it works offline.
  2. Press Start camera and read every code on this sheet, in any order.
  3. Check the SHA-256 on screen matches the one above, then press run the app. If it does not match, do not use it.
  4. Choose Scan SeedQR and read the metal plate. Check the fingerprint shown matches the one below.
  5. The first time, show the QR from Set up watch-only wallet to the wallet app.
  6. Make the transaction in the wallet app and read its QR with Scan PSBT.
  7. Check the recipient and amount, sign, and show the QR on screen to the wallet app to send it.
  8. Close the browser tab when done. The key is gone from that moment.
